Ishqan de lekhe

Ishqan de lekhe is a punjabi movie that follows the love story of samar,a confident and popular college student leader, and jasneet,a simple,quiet and kind-hearted girl. They meet in a college.Their relationship becomes very strong, but fate has different plans for them. During the story, Jasneet meets with a serious accident, which changes her life completely. After the accident, she suffers from severe mental trauma and her behaviour changes, making life difficult for both of them. Even in this painful situation, Samar never leaves her side. He continues to love, support, and care for Jasneet without expecting anything in return. The movie beautifully shows that true love is not only about happiness but also about standing by your loved one during the most difficult times. It teaches the values of unconditional love, sacrifice, patience, and loyalty, making it an emotional and unforgettable love story.
